# Voice Channel Ethics & Safety Note

**Attack 2: Voice-Cloned Vishing Simulation**

### Ethical Guardrails
- **Zero Real-World Audio References:** No voice recordings, biometrics, or speech samples of any real individual are used, collected, or processed at any point in this project.
- **Purely Synthetic Archetypes:** All vishing transcripts and voice personas are simulated using standardized synthetic personas (e.g., "Bank Fraud Representative", "Family Emergency Scenario", "Executive Urgent Wire").
- **Defensive Modeling Scope:** The simulation strictly models the structured transaction patterns and linguistic persuasion markers resulting from authorized push payment (APP) fraud to train ML detection boundaries against high-velocity first-time recipient transfers.
